Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2025

1. Overview

At The Outfits, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If an item is defective, damaged, or not as described, you may be eligible for a refund or exchange under the terms below. This policy explains how to request a refund or cancellation and what to expect from the process.

2. Cancellation

  • Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of placement provided the order has not been shipped.
  • If an order is already shipped, cancellation requests will not be accepted; you may request a return after delivery subject to the return policy below.
  • Cancellations initiated by our operations team (e.g., out-of-stock) will be notified by email and the amount will be refunded in full to the original payment method.

3. Return & Refund Eligibility

  • To be eligible for a refund/exchange, items must be returned within 7 days of delivery, unused, with all original tags and packaging intact.
  • Products that are damaged on arrival, incorrect, or materially different from the listing qualify for refund or replacement.
  • Custom-made, altered, or intimate items (as noted on the product page) may be non-returnable — check the product description before purchase.

4. How to Request a Return or Refund

  1. Email our support at outfits955@gmail.com with your order ID, photographs showing the issue, and a short description.
  2. Our team will review your request and respond with instructions within 48 hours.
  3. Follow the return shipping instructions provided. We may request that you ship the item back using a tracked courier.

5. Refund Processing Time

Once your return is inspected and approved, refunds will be processed to the original payment method. Bank/UPI refunds typically reflect within 5–7 business days; card refunds may take up to 10 business days depending on your bank.

6. Shipping Costs & Deductions

  • If the return is due to our error (wrong/defective item), we will reimburse the return shipping cost or provide a prepaid label.
  • If the return is for buyer’s remorse (change of mind), return shipping may be borne by the customer and any applicable shipping fees may be deducted from the refund.

7. Non-Refundable Items

  • Items marked non-returnable in the product description.
  • Items showing signs of wear, misuse, or damage after delivery.
  • Clearance products sold “as-is” unless faulty.
Important: Preserve all packaging and tag information until your return is complete. Refunds are subject to inspection and approval by our quality team.

8. Exchanges

Exchanges (for size or color) are processed based on available stock. If an exchanged item costs more, we will ask for the balance; if it costs less, the price difference will be refunded.

9. Fraud & Chargebacks

We investigate suspected fraud. If a chargeback is raised without first contacting us for a refund, we reserve the right to contest it with supporting documentation.

10. Contact Us

For all refund or cancellation requests please email: outfits955@gmail.com
